AI Usage Log

Tools Used: Google Gemini Pro

Usage Details:
I utilized Gemini primarily as an architectural sounding board and pair-programming assistant to accelerate boilerplate generation within the strict hackathon time constraints.

Specifically, I used AI to:

Design the initial PostgreSQL schema relationships (using SQLAlchemy) to ensure the models supported the complex business rules without hacks.

Scaffold the FastAPI routing structure and standard Pydantic validation envelopes.

Assist in writing the JWT authentication middleware to prevent immediate disqualification regarding unprotected endpoints and plaintext passwords.

Draft the structural methodology for the $O(n)$ in-memory matching algorithm, which I then refined to incorporate the specific completeness penalties and recency decay factors required by the prompt.

Modifications:
Code generated by the AI was heavily refactored to modularize it into separate routers (jobs.py, students.py, auth.py) rather than a monolithic file. I also manually adjusted the dependency injection pipelines to strict role-based constraints (e.g., separating get_current_student from get_current_company).
